### Smoke findings

Met in the development runs before the recorded attempt (whole-suite runs of `smoke.sh`, not recorded):

1. **MAIL-03, the app was wrong.** Settings › Email with a sender address and no sender name: the order email was
   not built at all (logged, never sent), though the form says an empty value falls back to the server's. Fixed in
   `891a71c` (each half of the sender falls back on its own), with
   `OrderEmailTest::testASenderAddressSavedWithoutANameTakesTheNameFromTheEnv`.
2. **UI-01, 04, 05, 07, 08, 09, 10, the app was wrong.** Targets under 44 px on a phone (the design's `--kf-target`):
   the scan note's and the slide-over's close buttons squeezed by long text, the cc add button, the react-select
   controls (38 px), "Remember me" (24 px), the user form's role names (21 px). Fixed in `f4cec8e` (CSS, touch screens
   only).
3. **INVC-06, the app cannot do what the old case said.** An account with only invoice roles answers 403 everywhere
   (no `ROLE_USER`), and Users cannot give `ROLE_USER`. Not fixed (the roles are production's): the README's known
   gaps; the case keeps the admin's refusal.
4. **INV-11, INVC-03, FLT-04, the test was wrong.** Uploading the every-product sheet sets every product's price to the
   sheet's 0 (as before), which the invoice cases in another lane read: INV-11 now uploads its own product's sheet.
5. **ORD-35, ORD-43, SHOP-08, the test setup was wrong.** With every lane at once, a pull from the fake shop (served by
   the same PHP) timed out at 15 s: the shops, comments, settings and webhook specs now run after the others, alone.
6. **Several, the tests were wrong.** Waits of 7 s too short for the dev stack under three lanes (now 15 s, all of
   them conditions); FLT-08 counted the rows before the filtered page arrived (now the server's total); MAIL-02 and
   SET-05 slept 5 s and 10 s (now a later email through the same queue, and a second admin); `prepare.sh` seeds
   FLT-05's customers in one statement (the API loop took 2.5 min).

## Conditions

The host ran other projects' Playwright suites at the same time; the stack's PHP (2 CPUs, a dev build with the
profiler and a debug log) was the bottleneck at about 200 % in every run. The ~8 minute target was not reached here
(9 min 23 s); raising `PHP_CPUS` (and `E2E_CPUS`) in the worktree's `.env`, or `pm.max_children` above 5, are the
levers, not tried (the stack's settings are the user's).
